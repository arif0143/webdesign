About Nimitt

Expertise That Moves Healthcare Forward.

Nimitt Consulting is a specialized healthcare consulting firm working at the intersection of complex therapeutics, strategic commercialization, and system-level transformation. We partner with cell and gene therapy manufacturers, biopharma companies, health systems, and payers to navigate the decisions that define their market position.
Where large generalist firms bring scale, we bring focus. Our engagements are led by senior practitioners who have worked inside the organizations we advise — bringing both strategic perspective and operational credibility to every challenge.

Specialized Expertise

Cell & Gene Therapy Is What We Do.

Cell and gene therapies represent the frontier of modern medicine — and the frontier of commercial complexity. We’ve worked alongside manufacturers from IND through post-launch, helping teams navigate the unique challenges of authorized treatment center networks, outcomes-based contracting, payer education, and patient journey design.
Our team brings direct experience with CAR-T, gene editing, and stem cell platforms, including engagements with multiple FDA-approved products and programs in late-stage development.
